Picture a day where you did nine of the ten things on your list, and they were hard. A strict to-do app would still call that a failure and keep your apps locked over the one that got away. That's a quick way to resent it. Veggies First has big days, so a day like that ends the way it should.

## What makes a day big

You pick the line, in Setup:

- **a fixed number** of veggies finished,
- **most of the plate**, 75% unless you change it,
- or **more than usual**, 150% of what you normally finish, also yours to change.

Cross the line and the app tells you, with the reward that's in play.

## What you get

You choose which rewards are on:

- **Call it a day.** What's left moves to tomorrow and dessert opens until midnight.
- **Shorter dessert breaks.**
- **Surprise bonuses.**

A clean sweep on a big day, with every veggie done and nothing pushed off, pays two veggies' worth of coins on top.

A big day also banks a rest pass: an extra day off to use once the week's own are spent.

## It's hard to game

The obvious trick would be to plan two things, do both, and call it "most of the plate". The app handles that in a few ways:

- "Most of the plate" counts the plate as you confirmed it the night before in the evening review, or as it stood at midnight. Adding or deleting to-dos during the day doesn't move the line.
- "More than usual" is measured against what you normally finish, so a short list doesn't pass.
- Changes to these settings wait for midnight, like the other rules.

## It tells you why your apps are open

After you call it a day, the bar at the top of the app and the lock screen say why dessert is open: which big-days setting you use, what the line came to today, and how many you finished. You don't have to wonder whether something broke.

## Make it suit you

If you always plan a little too much, that's fine. Lower the percentage, keep Call it a day on, and you'll have a system where finishing most of an ambitious list is a win. If you'd rather the bar be high, raise the number and treat a big day as a rare prize.

Either way the idea is the same. On ordinary days the rule is to finish the list. On a day when you clearly did more than your share, the app lets you stop.

## Try it

Leave big days on its starting setting for a week, then look at how often you hit it. Veggies First is free to try for 14 days. For planned rest, see [How to take a day off without wrecking your habits](/guides/days-off-and-weekend-mode/).
