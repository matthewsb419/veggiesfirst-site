Some days you need the app. You're waiting on a message, the video is for work, or you're just done. A blocker with no way through gets deleted the first time that happens. So Veggies First has passes: a way in without finishing a to-do, limited enough that it stays an exception.

## What a pass does

A pass opens your dessert apps for a stretch of time even though veggies are left on your plate. It gets you past both of the screens that stop you: the lock, and the break after a session.

You pick the daily plan in Setup: one long pass, or two short ones.

## Why it takes a hold

To use a pass you hold the button for several seconds. Let go early and it starts over. From the lock screen it's two taps with a wait between them.

That pause is the whole design. A few seconds is long enough to ask yourself whether you need this or just want it. The app's own notes put it plainly: even a short pause cuts how often people open distracting apps. You choose how long the hold is.

## The limits

- **No back-to-back passes.** After one, the next needs a finished veggie first.
- **They reset at midnight.** Today's unused passes don't carry over.
- **The plan can't change mid-day.** You can only switch between one long and two short on a day you haven't used a pass yet.
- **An empty plate gets none.** A day with nothing planned starts locked until you add a veggie or say you have nothing to do.

So a pass can rescue an afternoon. It can't quietly turn into how you use the app.

## Passes and the other ways out

Passes are for the unplanned. For the planned, there are better tools:

- **Weekend mode** is a whole day off, picked the day before.
- **The evening open window** opens your apps for an hour or two every night at a time you choose.
- **A big day** can open dessert once you've done far more than usual.

Using the right one keeps your passes for real surprises.

## In a household

On a kid's phone, the adults can set the pass plan and the length of the hold, or leave either for the kid to decide.

## Try it

Leave the pass plan on its starting setting for the first week and notice when you reach for one. Veggies First is free to try for 14 days. To see the screens a pass gets you past, read [What you see when you open a locked app](/guides/what-a-locked-app-shows/).
