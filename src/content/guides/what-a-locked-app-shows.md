When Veggies First is holding an app back, you don't get a blank wall. You get a screen that says why, what's left to do, and what your options are. There are six of them. Two stop you, and the other four let you through with a tap.

Each starts with the same word and color as the bar at the top of the app, so you can tell at a glance which one you're looking at.

![The lock screen on a dessert app: locked until you finish a veggie, with today's veggies listed.](/img/02-locked.jpg)

## The two that stop you

**Pink, LOCKED.** Veggies are left on today's plate and you have no coins. The screen lists what's left, your focus items first. Finish one, or use a pass with the small button.

**Orange, DESSERT BREAK.** A session just ended and dessert is resting. It says when dessert is back and what's waiting for you in the meantime. Wait it out, or use a pass.

If a session runs out in the middle of a video, the lock screen offers one or two more minutes to wrap up, once a session, paid for in coins. The break still happens afterwards, in full.

## The four that only tell you something

**Green, OPEN.** This is the time check. During a session it pops up to say how far in you are and how much is left. Tap OK and carry on. On a pass it's purple.

**Cream, REMINDER.** It's past your evening review time and tomorrow isn't planned. Plan now, or be reminded in 30 minutes.

**Pink, LOCKED until you plan today.** Today's plate has nothing on it. Add a veggie, or tap "I have nothing to do, life is easy" and dessert opens for the day.

**Green, PLATE CLEAN.** Every veggie is done and dessert is open for the rest of the day. It shows once a day if you've set up the optional Shortcuts automation, as a small well-done.

## Something to read while you wait

Every lock screen also shows a vegetable and one of its facts, stories or recipes. There are hundreds, from every continent, some rarer than others, and each one you see is collected as a badge. It's a small thing, but it makes a locked screen less of a slap.

## Why not just a block?

A plain block gives you nothing to do except look for a way round it. A screen that says "two things left: the dishes, and call Mom" gives you somewhere to go. Most of the time the fastest way to get your app back is to do the top thing on the list.
