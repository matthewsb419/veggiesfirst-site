You stop procrastinating on your phone by putting the task you're avoiding between you and the app you're avoiding it with. The phone is the easiest place to hide from a job, so the job has to come first, and the phone has to enforce it.

## Why the phone wins

The task feels big and has no clear start. The app is one tap away and starts at once. Every time you waver, the phone is there, so "five minutes" keeps happening.

Two things change that: make the task smaller, and make the app wait.

## Make the app wait

This is what Veggies First is for. Your to-dos are "veggies" and the apps you hide in are "dessert". While a veggie is left on today's list, a dessert app shows a lock screen in place of the feed, with the list on it.

So the moment you'd normally drift, you're shown the thing you're avoiding. It's a small jolt, and it comes at the right second.

## Make the task smaller

A to-do like "write the report" will sit there all day. "Write the first paragraph" gets done. In Veggies First each finished veggie pays coins, and a coin is a minute in your apps. So breaking a job into three small veggies means three payouts, and three reasons to start.

You can also drag your top items to the head of the list as your focus. They're shown in bold, and they come first on the lock screen.

## The break you take is a real one

When you finish something, you've earned time. That scrolling is a real break, because you've paid for it and you aren't hiding in it. It comes in sessions, with a short pause after each, so a break stays a break.

Clear the whole list and the apps are open for the rest of the day.

## The quieter fix the iPhone has

You can [turn off an app's notifications](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/ios), which stops the phone calling you over while you work. It's worth doing. It doesn't help with the times you pick the phone up yourself, though, and that's most of procrastination. A lock that points at your list is for those.

## If you'd rather not be stopped mid-task

Planned too much? There's an optional evening window that opens your apps for an hour or two at a time you set, done or not. And there are passes for the days you need in. The app is meant to push you into starting. It isn't meant to punish you.
