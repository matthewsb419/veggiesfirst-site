Most of Veggies First is gentle on purpose. It has passes, days off and praise for the good days. Some people don't want gentle. If a to-do without a deadline is one you'll start at 11:50 PM, there's Hardcore mode.

It's optional, and off until you turn it on.

## Level one: deadlines

With Hardcore mode on, every veggie gets a deadline: a usual time you pick, or its own time if you give it one.

A to-do finished after its deadline still has to get done for the plate to clear. It just pays no coins. You do the work and get none of the minutes. That's usually enough to get things moving before dinner.

The app is fair about it. A to-do you add after the day's deadline is due at 11:59 PM, so it doesn't start out late.

## Level two: super hardcore

This one charges you. Miss a deadline and that veggie's coins are taken from your balance the moment it passes.

- Your balance can go below zero.
- Your dessert apps stay locked until you've earned it back.
- The hole carries into the next day.
- Sunday night wipes it, so one terrible week doesn't follow you forever.

Turning the switch off closes the hole straight away. It's a tool you've chosen to use, and you can put it down.

## A hard stop on scrolling

Hardcore mode has a third switch, for people whose trouble is the amount they scroll and not their to-dos. Set a goal for your daily time in dessert apps, turn on "Lock dessert at my goal's limit", and once the day's time is used those apps lock until midnight. A clean plate doesn't open them, and neither does a pass.

Without that switch, a goal only keeps score.

## It comes with a look, and an attitude

Turn on Hardcore mode and a third look for the app appears: black paper, red ink, and vegetables with fangs. The Goals page changes its tone as well and teases you where it used to praise. It's a joke, and the numbers are the same.

## Who it's for

- People who work best against a clock.
- People who've got used to the normal rules and want the edge back.
- Anyone who has ever said they need someone to be strict with them.

## Who should skip it

If you're just getting started, leave it off. The standard rules, with to-dos first and your apps after, do plenty. Come back in a month if you want more pressure.
