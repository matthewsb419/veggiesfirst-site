You stop the morning scroll by deciding the night before what comes first, and by making sure the feed isn't available until it's done. In the morning you have the least willpower and the phone is closest, so the decision has to be made already.

## The night-before list

In Veggies First, tomorrow's plate is set tonight. You add tomorrow's to-dos under Tomorrow & backlog, and at midnight they become today's. An evening review can remind you at a time you pick, walk you through what's left of today, and then ask for tomorrow's list.

Put one easy thing at the top: make the bed, drink water, ten push-ups. It only has to be something you can do before you're properly awake.

## What the morning looks like

You wake up and reach for the phone. The apps you marked as dessert are locked, because nothing on today's plate is done yet. In place of the feed you see the list, with that easy thing first.

![The lock screen on a dessert app: locked until you finish a veggie, with today's veggies listed.](/img/02-locked.jpg)

Do it and tick it off. You've earned some minutes, and the day has started with something done.

## What if I didn't plan?

Then the plate is empty, and an empty plate at the start of the day is locked too, with no passes, until you add a veggie. So a morning with no plan still starts with you writing one. Reminders go off while tomorrow's plate is still empty, to make that rare.

If today really is a nothing day, the lock screen has a button for that: "I have nothing to do, life is easy". Tap it and dessert opens.

## Messages still work

You choose what's locked. If you need to see messages or your calendar first thing, don't mark them as dessert. Lock the two or three apps that eat the first half hour, and leave the rest alone.

## The iPhone setting that helps

You can [change which notifications reach you](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/ios), so there's less on the screen pulling you in when you turn off the alarm. That takes away the bait. Veggies First handles the other half, which is what happens once you unlock the phone anyway.
