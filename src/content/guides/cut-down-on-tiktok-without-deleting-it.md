Deleting TikTok works until the evening you reinstall it. If you like the app and just want less of it, keep it, and change when it opens and how a sitting ends.

## Make it open after something, not before

In Veggies First, you mark TikTok as "dessert" and put your to-dos for the day on a list. While anything is left on the list, opening TikTok shows a lock screen with the list on it.

![The lock screen on a dessert app: locked until you finish a veggie, with today's veggies listed.](/img/02-locked.jpg)

Finish a to-do and you earn minutes. So TikTok still happens every day, after the dishes or the assignment, and it's no longer how you put them off.

## Give each sitting an end

The feed will never tell you to stop. Veggies First runs your minutes in sessions, and when one is used up there's a short break before the next. You look up. The lock screen shows what's left on your list. Often that's the moment you'd have wanted an hour ago.

You set how long a session and a break are. The numbers can only change at midnight, so you can't stretch tonight's session while you're in it.

## Know where you are

A time check can pop up mid-scroll to show how far into the session you are. One tap and it's gone. It's there because time on TikTok is hard to feel.

## Then let yourself enjoy it

Finish the whole list and TikTok is open for the rest of the day, with no timer. The aim was less of the scrolling you didn't choose, and a guilt-free evening after a finished list is the scrolling you did choose.

## Keep score if that helps

Set a goal, like under two hours a day in your dessert apps, and the app tracks how many days you make it, with a calendar of the last weeks. Going over locks nothing by default. It only shows in your history.

## What about the iPhone's own limit?

You can [set a daily time allowance in Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-schedules-and-time-allowances-iphb0c7313c9/ios) for social apps. It's a good first step if you've never tried one. Many people find it becomes a countdown they wave away at the worst time. Veggies First starts from what you've done, and it's built to be hard to wave away.

## It works for the others too

Pick the whole Social category, or add the websites, and Reels and Shorts follow the same rules. The minutes are shared across everything you mark, so swapping one feed for another doesn't get you more.
