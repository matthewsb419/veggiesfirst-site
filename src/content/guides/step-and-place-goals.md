The easiest to-do to fudge is one where you're the only judge. "Go for a walk" gets ticked after a trip to the mailbox. Your phone can count some things for you, though, and in Veggies First those to-dos tick themselves when they're really done.

## Step goals

A step goal is a to-do that Apple's Health app counts. You set the number, and it can't be checked off by hand. It finishes itself when you reach the goal.

Until then, the apps you've marked as dessert stay locked, like with any unfinished to-do. So you earn your scrolling by walking.

There's a bonus as well. On a day with a step goal, every mile you walk earns more coins on top.

Steps update when you open the app, and you can let them update in the background so the goal checks itself off while the phone's in your pocket.

## Place goals

A place goal pays for time spent somewhere: the gym, the library, the studio. You pick the place by searching or by using where you are, and set how long.

It pays a coin for each minute you're there, up to the goal, and then checks itself off. You can let extra minutes keep paying, so a long session at the gym earns a long evening.

The phone notices you arriving and leaving even with the app closed. That needs location access set to Always, and the app asks for it when you make your first place goal.

## Why these are worth setting up

- **They can't be faked with a tap.** Nobody has to trust anybody, including you.
- **They pay in proportion.** More walking or more time there earns more.
- **They need no proof.** If you use a friend as a reviewer for other to-dos, these prove themselves and pay in full.

## Make them repeat

A step goal every day, or the gym three times a week, can be a repeating to-do so it lands on your plate by itself. Track it as a goal and you get its history and a widget too.

## A note on privacy

Steps come from Health on your phone. A place goal uses your location to tell when you arrive at the spot you picked and when you leave.

## What they don't suit

They can't count anything the phone can't measure. Reading, homework and the dishes still need a tap, or a photo to a friend if you want a witness.
