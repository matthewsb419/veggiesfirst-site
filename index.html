How it works

Eat your veggies before dessert.

It's easy to lose hours to social media, short videos and games while the things that keep your life running wait. Veggies First puts them back in the right order.

1

Your to-dos are veggies

Plan tomorrow's plate tonight. At midnight it becomes today's list.

2

The apps you pick are dessert

Single apps, whole categories or websites. While veggies are left, dessert stays locked.

3

Finish one, earn time

Every veggie earns dessert minutes. Clear the plate and the rest of the day is yours.

Moderation, not a ban

Dessert waits until the veggies are done.

It isn't about giving up the apps you like. Take care of what matters, then enjoy your screen time without it eating the day.

  • Rule changes take effect tomorrow, not in the moment.
  • A few passes for when you really need one.
  • An empty plate stays locked, so there's no skipping the planning.

Time you can see

Finish one. Earn your time.

Dessert time is counted in minutes you actually use. A quick check-in shows how far into a session you are, and when it's up, dessert takes a short break.

Hard to cheat

Proof for the ones that matter.

A photo, a screenshot, a short video, a walking route or a note, checked by someone you choose. Your reviewer doesn't need the app: they get a link by text.

  • Step goals count themselves from Apple Health.
  • Time at a place, like an hour at the gym or the library.

For households

Help your kids build good habits.

Chores and homework first, then screen time. Parents hand out veggies, set what each one earns and review the proof. Kids see what's theirs to do and what it unlocks.

For students

A syllabus in, veggies out.

Hand a photo or PDF of a syllabus, a schedule or a chore list to the AI you already use. Paste back the list it writes and every task lands on the right day.

Questions

Good to know.

What is Veggies First?

Veggies First is an iPhone to-do list that doubles as an app blocker. You pick the apps that eat your time, and they stay locked until today's to-dos are done. Each to-do you finish earns minutes on those apps.

How does it help me stop doomscrolling?

The apps you scroll are locked at the start of the day. To open them you finish something on your list first, and the time you earn runs out, so a quick look can't turn into hours.

Does it block apps completely?

No. It's built for moderation. You choose which apps, categories or websites count as dessert, how many minutes each to-do earns, and once the list is clear the rest of the day is yours.

Can I just turn it off when I'm tempted?

Rule changes take effect tomorrow, not in the moment, so the choice you made with a clear head holds. There are a few passes for the days you really need one.

Does it work for kids and families?

Yes. In a household, parents hand out chores and homework, set what each one earns and review the proof. One Household plan covers up to six people.

How much does it cost?

It's free to try for 14 days. After that it's $4.99 a month, $47.99 a year or $99 once for a lifetime plan. A Household plan for up to six people is $9.99 a month or $95.99 a year.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Veggies First is for iPhone, and it uses Apple's Screen Time to lock apps.

What happens to my data?

Your to-dos, Screen Time choices, steps and location live on your phone. There are no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sold. You can delete your account in the app at any time.
