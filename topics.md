# The posting schedule

Six posts a week, never on a Tuesday (Ben, 2026-11-26), for a year.

- **Wednesday, Friday, Sunday:** a guide written ahead. Each is dated and goes out by itself at 8 PM Central on its day (the site rebuilds every evening). The thirty below run to February 3, 2027; a new batch is needed before then.
- **Monday, Thursday, Saturday:** a guide the job writes that morning from what Search Console shows, or from "Extra topics" below while there's nothing to go on. It opens a pull request; merged by 8 PM, it goes out that evening.

All of them follow `agent/style.md`. The full plan is `Docs/seo-guide-site.md` in the app repo.
